# Game Design Document Example
## Project: "Chronicles of Aethelgard"

### Overview
A fantasy RPG where players explore the ancient kingdom of Aethelgard, uncovering secrets of a lost civilization while navigating political intrigue between rival noble houses.

### Characters

#### 1. Elara Windrider
- **Role**: Protagonist, Royal Scout
- **Personality**: Curious, Brave, Loyal to the Crown
- **Goals**: Uncover the truth about her parents' disappearance, protect the kingdom from internal threats
- **Arc**: Starts as a novice scout, becomes a key figure in preventing a civil war

#### 2. Lord Valerius Blackwood
- **Role**: Antagonist, Head of House Blackwood
- **Personality**: Ambitious, Cunning, Charismatic
- **Goals**: Seize the throne, restore Blackwood family to power, eliminate rivals
- **Arc**: Revealed to be manipulated by an ancient evil, faces redemption or destruction choice

#### 3. Seraphina Moonshadow
- **Role**: Mentor, Last of the Moon Druids
- **Personality**: Wise, Mysterious, Protective of ancient knowledge
- **Goals**: Preserve balance between nature and civilization, train Elara
- **Arc**: Sacrifices herself to seal the ancient evil, or survives to guide new generation

### Main Story Beats

#### Act 1: The Scout's Calling
1. Elara discovers strange artifacts in the Whispering Woods
2. Reports to the Royal Court, meets Lord Blackwood
3. Assigned to investigate ancient ruins with Seraphina
4. Uncovers first clue about parents' disappearance

#### Act 2: Shadows of the Past
1. Political tensions rise between noble houses
2. Elara learns Blackwood family's dark secret
3. Seraphina reveals the true nature of the ancient threat
4. Elara must choose: loyalty to crown or pursuit of truth

#### Act 3: The Final Convergence
1. Ancient evil awakens, threatening entire kingdom
2. Elara unites rival houses against common enemy
3. Final confrontation at the Heart of Aethelgard
4. Multiple endings based on player choices:
   - Peaceful resolution: Evil sealed, kingdom united
   - Sacrificial ending: Elara sacrifices herself
   - Dark ending: Blackwood succeeds, becomes new ruler
   - Balance ending: Ancient power harnessed for good

### Branching Narrative Points

#### Decision 1: The Blackwood Alliance
- **Option A**: Trust Lord Blackwood's offer of alliance
  - Gain political support, but risk betrayal
  - Unlocks "Political Intrigue" branch
- **Option B**: Reject alliance, work alone
  - Maintain independence, harder challenges
  - Unlocks "Lone Wolf" branch

#### Decision 2: Ancient Knowledge
- **Option A**: Share discoveries with Seraphina
  - Gain druidic powers, understand ancient magic
  - Unlocks "Druidic Path" branch
- **Option B**: Keep knowledge secret
  - Maintain human abilities, rely on skill
  - Unlocks "Mortal Path" branch

#### Decision 3: Final Confrontation
- **Option A**: Confront ancient evil directly
  - Epic battle, potential for heroic sacrifice
  - Leads to Sacrificial or Heroic endings
- **Option B**: Use political manipulation
  - Turn houses against each other, clever victory
  - Leads to Political or Cunning endings
- **Option C**: Seek balance and understanding
  - Attempt to harmonize with ancient power
  - Leads to Balance or Enlightenment endings

### Technical Notes
- Each character has 3-5 major decision points
- Story branches reconverge at key plot moments
- Endings are consequential based on accumulated choices
- Supporting characters have their own mini-arcs that intersect with main story
